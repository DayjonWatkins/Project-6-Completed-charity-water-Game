How to Play & Game Elements

+1

Clean Water Drops

Catch clean drops to add +1 point and fill the Clean Water Reservoir. Drops disappear immediately upon tapping.

Jerry Can Bonus

Catch the floating yellow jerry can for +3 bonus points! Disappears in a golden burst.

-1

Contaminated Drops

Avoid murky red/orange drops! Catching one subtracts -1 point from your reservoir.

Difficulty Modes

Easy 15 drops · 40s
Normal 20 drops · 30s
Hard 25 drops · 22s
Accessibility: Click, tap, or use Tab to select falling drops and press Space or Enter to catch them.
